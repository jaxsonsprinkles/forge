The Valley Observatory was founded in 1889 by Dr. Miriam Halloway on Beacon Hill, overlooking Thornwick Valley. It housed the largest refracting telescope in Cobalt County at the time, with a 12-inch lens imported from a manufacturer in Port Colston. Dr. Halloway trained a small staff of assistants over the years. The observatory is best known for the discovery of Halloway's Comet in 1894.
