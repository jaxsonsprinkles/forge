The Thornwick Valley Harvest Festival began in 1895 as a small gathering to celebrate the autumn barley harvest. It has been held every October since, except during a two-year suspension from 1918 to 1919. The festival's signature drink is Cobalt Amber, supplied each year by the Thornwick Brewery, and its signature dish is river trout, supplied by the Adder River Fishing Guild. Attendance grew from around 200 people in its first year to over 3,000 by 1950.
