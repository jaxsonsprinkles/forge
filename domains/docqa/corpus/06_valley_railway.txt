The Valley Railway line connecting Thornwick Valley to the city of Port Colston opened in 1902. The line runs 42 miles and includes three stations: Thornwick Station, Millbrook Station, and Port Colston Terminal. Thornwick Station was the line's only stop within Cobalt County and quickly became the town's primary link to outside trade. Freight traffic on the line, mostly silver ore, doubled within its first five years of operation.
