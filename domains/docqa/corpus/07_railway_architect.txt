The stations on the Valley Railway line, including Thornwick Station, were designed by architect Nathaniel Pryce. Pryce, who trained in Port Colston before returning to Cobalt County, was known for his use of red brick and steep gabled roofs, a style he also applied to several public buildings in Thornwick Valley, including the town's schoolhouse. Pryce died in 1911, and Thornwick Station was renamed the Pryce Memorial Depot in his honor in 1912.
