Miller's Crossing, the stone bridge spanning the Adder River at the Narrows, was completed in 1871 under the direction of civil engineer Agatha Voss. It was the first stone arch bridge in Cobalt County and cost 14,000 dollars to build, funded largely by a donation from Rowan Thornwick. The bridge replaced the rope ferry that had previously served that crossing, and it remains in use today, though it was reinforced with steel supports in 1965.
