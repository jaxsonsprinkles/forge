Thornwick Valley was founded in 1842 by Elias Thornwick, a fur trader who settled on the eastern bank of the Adder River. The original settlement consisted of forty founding families who built the first cabins near what is now the town square. Elias named the settlement after his father, Cormac Thornwick, and the town was formally incorporated into Cobalt County in 1849. Elias Thornwick served as the town's first mayor until his death in 1867, at which point his son, Rowan Thornwick, took over as the second mayor. Rowan's own children included Delia Thornwick, who would go on to found the town's brewery, and Wren Thornwick, who later ran the family's mining interests.
